# ป้ายเมนูไทย (Thai Menu Sign)

A single-page guide to popular Thai dishes: visitors browse a library of dishes, get a random pick for "today," and save personal favorites.

## Language

**Dish**:
A single Thai food item in the library, carrying a name, region, category, and spice index.
_Avoid_: Menu item, entry, food

**Dish Library**:
The full set of dishes the page draws from, for both browsing and random selection. Every dish in it is shown on the page — nothing is held back from view.
_Avoid_: Menu, catalog, list

**Signature Dish**:
A dish singled out within the library as an especially iconic, must-try pick, marked with a distinct badge.
_Avoid_: Featured dish, highlighted item

**Spice Index**:
A 0–3 rating of how spicy a dish is, shown as filled vs. unfilled chili marks.
_Avoid_: Heat level, spiciness rating

**Today's Dish**:
The dish currently surfaced by the random picker. A new pick replaces it on every click, but a dish already surfaced earlier the same local calendar day is excluded until the whole library has cycled through, after which repeats are allowed again.
_Avoid_: Random pick, daily special

**Favorites**:
The set of dishes a visitor has personally saved on their own device. Private to that browser — not shared or synced with other visitors or devices.
_Avoid_: Saved items, likes, bookmarks
